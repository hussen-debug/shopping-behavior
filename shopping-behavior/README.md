# shopping-behavior

## Analytical Questions

What are 6 analytical questions you can ask about this dataset?



1. Do customers who use promo codes also tend to purchase more frequently, or do they show up less often and rely on discounts to shop?
2. Which age groups generate the highest average purchase amount, and does that pattern shift by season?
3. Is there a relationship between a customer's number of previous purchases and the review rating they leave — do loyal customers rate higher?
4. Which payment methods are most popular among customers who shop weekly versus those who shop annually?
5. Do male and female customers differ in the types of items they buy most, and does that difference change by season?
6. Which U.S. states produce the highest total purchase volume, and are those same states more likely to use express shipping?
